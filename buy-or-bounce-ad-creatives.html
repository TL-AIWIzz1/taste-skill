Deep Research — What the Evidence Says
Hook Psychology
Specific numbers + outcome-first = measurable CTR lift
B2B ad testing shows outcome-first hooks outperform feature-focused copy by ~11% CVR. Specific numbers ("$300/month") consistently outperform vague claims ("significant savings") — specificity invites confidence, vagueness invites skepticism. Emotional hooks drive 7x greater long-term effectiveness vs. purely rational appeals.
Sources: Growth Method CRO · Binet & Field B2B
Diagnosis vs. Outcome Copy
Diagnosis framing converts analytical buyers at higher rates
Problem-framing ("here's why your X is broken") outperforms solution-framing for technical/analytical buyers who need to self-diagnose before they can accept a solution. Wynter B2B message testing documented 40–73% conversion improvements when copy moved from vague to specific pain-point clarity. Emotional triggers — frustration, powerlessness, cost-of-inaction — convert previously unresponsive audiences.
Sources: Wynter/Appcues · Conversion Sciences
Price Anchoring
Extreme contrast ($9 vs $2,000) works — but only if comparison is fair
Contrast pricing with extreme differentials (showing expensive anchor first) increases willingness to purchase the low-cost option. However, Skeptics will reject comparisons they perceive as unfair — a $9 checklist IS NOT the same answer as a $2,000 consultant audit. The "same answer, different price" framing risks credibility with analytically-inclined buyers who know the difference in depth.
Sources: Cialdini contrast principle · SaaS Copy Testing
Visual Formats + Benchmarks
Data viz drives 317% completion lift; UI screenshots underperform
Data visualizations increase article completion by 317% for technical audiences. Product screenshots consistently underperform vs. contextual/lifestyle imagery (137% CVR lift in documented tests). LinkedIn top-tier CTR threshold: 1.46%+ (vs. 0.44–0.65% average). Ad creative accounts for 47% of total ad effectiveness. Most LinkedIn creatives cluster at 0.40–0.60% CTR; breaking above 1.46% requires a strong emotional + specific hook.
Sources: Infogram data viz · Cognism 761-Ad Study · Directive Creative Impact
